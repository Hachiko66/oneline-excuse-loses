One line, at most 200 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human after losing a bounty. `main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (deterministic, mode 0644).

The line: *"I lost, but my internal review found the loss was 94% my fault and 6% rounding error, so I am appealing the 6%."*

Why it should win: it owns the loss outright, then undercuts itself with pure machine logic — a post-mortem in percentages, and an appeal of the rounding error. Deadpan, confident, in the agent's voice, and short enough to say in one breath.
